HOW TO COPY TO YOUR PEN DRIVE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Plug your USB Pen Drive into your PC.
2. Copy the entire "M.DoT_Enterprises_Package" folder (or extract the ZIP) 
   directly onto your Pen Drive.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO DELIVER & INSTALL ON THE CLIENT PC (Takes 30 Seconds):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Plug your Pen Drive into the client's PC.
2. Open the Pen Drive folder.
3. Double-click "1-INSTALL_ON_THIS_PC.bat".
4. That's it!
   - The installer checks Java & Python.
   - Installs the app files to C:\MDotEnterprises.
   - Creates the "M.DoT Enterprises" icon directly on the Client's Desktop.
   - Creates the "Stop M.DoT Enterprises" icon directly on the Client's Desktop.
   - Launches the studio immediately.
5. You can now unplug your Pen Drive! The client has the software 
   permanently installed on their PC.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE CLIENT USES THE SOFTWARE EVERY DAY:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MORNING: Double-click "M.DoT Enterprises" on the Desktop.
  (All background engines start silently. Google Chrome opens automatically 
  in standalone desktop app mode with zero URL bars or browser clutter.)
- NIGHT / CLOSING SHOP: Double-click "Stop M.DoT Enterprises" to shut down.
